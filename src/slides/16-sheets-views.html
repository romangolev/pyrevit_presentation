Sheets & Views Tools

  • Batch Sheet Maker — batch-create sheets from a list
  • Rename Selected Views — bulk rename with find & replace
  • Rename Selected Sheets — batch-rename sheet numbers / names
  • Add Views to Sheets — place views on sheets in bulk
  • Copy Sheets to Open Documents — transfer sheets across models
Most tools open a PyRevit forms dialog — no WPF XAML required.
These are among the most used tools on real projects. Batch Sheet Maker alone saves hours of manual bookkeeping.
